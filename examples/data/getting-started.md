# Getting started with your Lumen thermostat

Lumen makes two smart thermostats: the LM-200 and the LM-300 Pro. Both work with
most 24V heating and cooling systems.

## Installation

Turn off power to your HVAC system at the breaker before removing your old
thermostat. Label each wire with the stickers included in the box, then connect
the wires to the matching terminals on the Lumen base plate. The LM-300 Pro needs
a C-wire (common wire); the LM-200 can run on its built-in battery for up to
six months without one.

## First-time setup

Download the Lumen app, create an account, and tap "Add device". The thermostat
shows a six-digit pairing code on its screen. Enter the code in the app and choose
your home Wi-Fi network. Setup usually takes about ten minutes.

## Schedules

Open the Schedule tab in the app to set comfort temperatures for each part of the
day. The LM-300 Pro also learns your routine automatically during the first week.

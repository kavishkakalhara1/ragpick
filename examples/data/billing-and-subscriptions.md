# Billing and subscriptions

Lumen thermostats work without a subscription. Lumen Plus is an optional
subscription that adds energy reports, extended 2-year history, and priority
support.

Lumen Plus costs $4.99 per month or $49 per year. You can cancel at any time
under Account > Subscription; you keep access until the end of the billing period.

Invoices are emailed after every payment and are also available under
Account > Subscription > Invoices. We accept major credit cards and PayPal.
Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase.

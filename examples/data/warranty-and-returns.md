# Warranty and returns

Every Lumen thermostat comes with a two-year limited warranty from the date of
purchase. The LM-300 Pro warranty extends to three years when the device is
registered in the app within 30 days of purchase.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and hardware failures, including sensor
faults (error E-3301). It does not cover damage from incorrect wiring, water
damage, or installation by unlicensed contractors where a license is required.

## Returns

Unused products can be returned within 60 days for a full refund. To start a
return, open Account > Orders in the app or contact support with your order
number. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business
days after the return is received.

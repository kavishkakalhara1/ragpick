# Account and password

## Reset your password

On the sign-in screen of the Lumen app, tap "Forgot password" and enter the email
address on your account. You will receive a reset link that is valid for 30
minutes. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder or request a new
link.

## Two-factor authentication

Enable two-factor authentication under Account > Security. Lumen supports
authenticator apps and SMS codes.

## Sharing access

Invite family members under Account > Home members. Members can change the
temperature, but only the account owner can remove devices or change billing.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account under Account > Privacy. Deletion is permanent and
removes all devices, schedules, and energy history after 14 days.

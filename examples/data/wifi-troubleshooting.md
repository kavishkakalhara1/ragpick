# Wi-Fi troubleshooting

Lumen thermostats connect to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks only. 5 GHz-only networks are
not supported.

If the thermostat shows "Offline" in the app:

1. Check that your router is on and other devices can reach the internet.
2. On the thermostat, open Settings > Network and select "Reconnect".
3. Move the router closer or add a range extender if the signal shows one bar.
4. Restart the thermostat by holding the dial for 10 seconds.

Networks that require a web login page (captive portals), such as hotel or
university Wi-Fi, cannot be used. If your router uses the same name for 2.4 GHz
and 5 GHz bands, temporarily disable the 5 GHz band during setup.

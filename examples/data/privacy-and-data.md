# Privacy and data

Lumen stores temperature readings, schedules, and device status to provide the
service. Location is used only for geofencing (auto-away), and only if you turn
it on.

We never sell personal data. Energy history is kept for 2 years with Lumen Plus
and 90 days without it.

You can download a copy of your data under Account > Privacy > Export data. The
export is a ZIP file of CSV files and is ready within 24 hours.
